## 🧩 About

**World of Warcraft Overlay** is a comprehensive overlay menu for WoW retail and Classic featuring HUD tracking, rotation helper, cooldown timers, map waypoints, bag manager, and nameplate tools.

Based on popular addons like **WeakAuras**, **ElvUI**, and **Details!**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Tools
- HUD Tracker — monitor health, resources, and target info
- Rotation Helper — visual priority prompts for your spec
- Cooldown Timers — track major abilities and trinkets
- Nameplate Tools — enhance enemy and ally nameplates
- Combat Log Reader — parse and display recent events

### 🎨 Interface & Overlays
- Custom Overlay Skins — adjust colors, fonts, and layouts
- Map Waypoints — pin and navigate to custom coordinates
- Bag Manager — sort, search, and organize inventory
- Minimap Buttons — quick access to all overlay panels

### 🏋️ Utility & Training
- Training Dummy Mode — simulated rotations and DPS readouts
- Gold Tracker — record income and expenses per session
- Raid Notes — annotate boss strategies inside the overlay
- Macro Import — load and bind custom Lua macros
